It starts with a good idea. Then the work goes everywhere: the draft, the chat, the task list, the review. Bring it together in CoWrite, a shared space for ideas to become work.

One draft. Many minds. Write together live. See Ada and Bea’s cursors, and keep Tomi’s feedback attached to the sentence it is about. Suggest a better phrase, accept the change, and sign off on the work.

Meet Nib, your writing assistant inside the work. Ask for a clearer introduction. Nib proposes a change; you choose whether to accept. AI suggests. You decide.

A conversation becomes a decision, and a decision becomes a next step. Keep what was decided, who decided, and the discussion behind it. Assign the task to Ada, set Friday as the due date, and keep it linked to the guide.

Every version is still there to compare and restore. Keep writing offline; edits merge when you reconnect. Publish the finished guide, present it as slides, or export to Word and PDF.
