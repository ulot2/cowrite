Meet CoWrite. One space. From idea to done. Collect an idea, talk it through, and keep the conversation with the work.

Write together live, see named cursors, and keep feedback in the text. Comment, suggest changes, and sign off sections so everyone knows when the draft is approved.

Record what was decided, who decided, and why. Each decision connects to the original discussion. Turn words into assigned tasks with dates, linked to the work.
