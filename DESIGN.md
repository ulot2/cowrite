# Design notes

The look is "Studio": a white page, a gray tint only on the things you can pick up (cards, the search field, the active item), one cobalt accent, Manrope for everything except the italic serif wordmark. Light and dark follow the system setting, and the account menu can pin either one.

## Tokens

All values live on `:root` in `app/app.css`. Dark redefines the same names under `prefers-color-scheme: dark` (unless the page is pinned light) and under `[data-theme="dark"]`. Screens use the token names, never raw values.

| Token | Use |
|---|---|
| `--bg` | The page, the top bar, the sidebar |
| `--tint`, `--tint-2` | Cards, the search field, the active sidebar item, hover; `-2` is the hover of a tinted thing |
| `--fg`, `--fg-muted` | Text, and secondary text (both pass 4.5:1) |
| `--border` | Hairlines only: the top bar, the sidebar, dividers |
| `--accent`, `--accent-hover`, `--accent-soft`, `--accent-fg` | The one accent: primary button, active icon, focus ring; `-soft` is the focus wash, `-fg` the text on the accent |
| `--ok`, `--warn`, `--warn-soft`, `--danger` | Status colors. Never decoration. |
| `--shadow` | Only floating things: the account menu, the phone drawer |
| `--radius`, `--radius-lg` | 8 px controls, 12 px cards and menus |
| `--topbar`, `--sidebar`, `--rail`, `--measure` | 3.5rem bar, 14rem sidebar, 3.5rem collapsed rail, 42rem text column |
| `--font`, `--font-display` | Manrope, Instrument Serif (wordmark only) |
| `--t-fast`, `--t-base`, `--ease-out`, `--ease-in` | 150 ms for hover and focus, 220 ms for enter, exit, and the sidebar |

## Motion

Only `opacity`, `transform`, and the sidebar's grid column animate. Enter is a fade with a 6 px rise (`rise`) or drop (`drop`); avatars scale in (`pop`); pages fade; cards stagger by 30 ms. Everything is off under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## Parts

- Top bar: wordmark, search (goes to Documents with `?q=`), the one primary button, and the account avatar. The avatar opens a native `<details>` menu with name, email, the theme choice, and sign out.
- Sidebar: Home and Documents with line icons, and a collapse control at the bottom. Collapsed, it is an icon rail. The state is saved per browser and applied before the first paint. On phones it is a drawer behind a menu button.
- Home: a greeting and the six most recent documents. Documents: all of them, with search and delete.
- Cards: title, the first lines of the text (written by the document object a few seconds after an edit), when it changed, who is on it.
- Document page: breadcrumb, presence avatars top right (a green dot means that person has a cursor in the text), a quiet pill only while connecting or offline, the title in Manrope at 2.5rem, the text on the page itself.
- Editor: BlockNote with its colors mapped to our tokens (`.bn-container` in `app.css`), so menus and the toolbar match both themes. The other user's cursor label stays visible; the library only shows it on hover.
- Comments: a "Comments" button with the open count in the presence row opens a panel beside the text (a sheet from the bottom on phones) with an Open / Resolved switch. Commented text carries the accent wash; the selected thread's text the warning wash. The library's icon-only actions get accessible names from a small observer.
- Login: a two-column page, statement left, tinted form card right.

- Share: a native `<dialog>` from the "Share" button in the presence row. Members with a role select, add by email, the link with copy and revoke, the space select. A space page uses the same header row as a document (breadcrumb, then the public switch and Share as icon tools), an eyebrow that says private or public, the name, and a meta line with the members' avatars and "New document".
- Sidebar: a "Spaces" group under Documents, one link per space and a small "New space" field.
- Cards: the space name in the accent color above the preview when the document is in a space.
- History: the same one-row header as the document (breadcrumb, "Open the document"), then a "Version history" eyebrow in the accent and the document title. Versions are a timeline on the left (a dot per version on a hairline, the selected one filled with the accent) with the "Name this version" field above; when the page is narrow (by its own width, so a tablet with the sidebar counts) the timeline becomes a strip of chips that scrolls sideways, long names cut with an ellipsis, so the text stays near the top. The chosen version sits on a sheet (white, hairline, soft shadow) with the author's avatar, "Compare to" and "Restore". The diff is a list of blocks: removed on the warning wash with a strike, added on the accent wash, each with a text label so color is not the only signal.
- Document header: on the left, "‹ Documents" and the status pill (Idea in the accent, Draft grey, In review warning, Approved green, Done muted with a check; owners and reviewers open it for the moves they may make). The title is not repeated there; it is the big heading below. On the right: the presence avatars (with a short pulsing "Connecting" or "Offline" pill when needed), then two outlined groups of icon tools with tooltips (Suggest, Ask Nib, Outline, Comments; then History and Export), then Share as the one filled accent button. A pressed tool turns accent on the accent wash. When the row is too narrow it wraps instead of overlapping. On phones: "‹ Documents" and the pill on top, the tools spread across a second row, Share as an icon.
- Suggestions: insertions on the accent wash with an accent underline, deletions struck on the warning wash, changed formatting with a dotted underline. Hovering a suggestion shows a small card under it: "Suggested by Bea" with Accept and Reject. A quiet bar above the text says how many suggestions there are and, while the cursor is in one, who made it, with Accept and Reject; otherwise Accept all and Reject all.
- Mentions: "@Name" in a comment, accent text on the accent wash, picked from a menu of the members that opens on `@`.
- Reading view: the document outside the editor, at the measure, in the same type as the editor. Public page: only the wordmark above and "Written with cowrite" below, the title, the published date. Print: the same page with a hint bar that stays off paper.
- Slides: one idea per screen, the heading large and fluid (`clamp`), the bar fades away in full screen and comes back on hover or focus, a counter below, notes on the warning wash.
- More menu (⋯): Present and the four exports, the same popover as the status menu. Outline: the side panel lists the headings, indented by level, level 1 in bold.
- Search results: the matched words on the accent wash inside the card, "In a comment" when the hit is there.
- Task block: a checkbox, the text, a quiet assignee dropdown, and a date; done tasks are struck through in the muted color. Decision block: a tinted callout with "D-12" and a status dropdown; decided has a green edge, dropped is struck through.
- Tasks page: groups in planning order (Overdue in red, This week, Later, No date, Done), one hairline row per task with a round tick that fills green.
- Board: tinted columns that scroll sideways (85% of the screen on phones), white cards that lift on hover and tilt while dragged, an up-vote that turns accent when it is yours, a card menu with Move to, Turn into (a task, a document), and Delete.
- Nib (the AI assistant): an accent "✦ Ask Nib" button leads the selection toolbar, the header has a Nib tool, and `@nib` in the text shows one card under the cursor ("Ask Nib: <instruction>", Enter sends; no card when "@" is not followed by "nib", so email addresses stay quiet). All open one menu: next to the selection or cursor on a wide screen (under the header tool when opened there), a bottom sheet over a scrim on a phone. On top, an instruction field on the tint that grows with the text, with a round accent send button; below, three groups (Edit selection, Write, Whole document). The running command shows the spinner; when the answer lands the menu closes and selects the new suggestion, which reads "Suggested by Nib". In comments, Nib is the last name in the @ list.
- Suggestion bar: a card that sticks under the top bar while you scroll, lined up with the text: a round badge (a pencil, or the sparkle for Nib), "Suggestion 2 of 5 · by Bea" or "5 suggestions", previous and next in a small outlined pair, then Reject (outlined, red on hover) and Accept (filled green), or Reject all and Accept all. The hover card over a suggestion uses the same two buttons. On phones the bar sits at the bottom of the screen with two wide buttons.
- Settings: a page at the width of a wide column, section links on the left (sticky; "Delete account" in red) and one card per section on the right: Profile, Account, Appearance, Notifications, Nib, Delete account. Each card has a title and a one-line purpose, then hairline rows: the name and a plain explanation on the left, the control on the right (a switch, a segmented control, swatches, or a field with its button). Switches and swatches save at once; forms with a button save on the button, and each shows "Saved" in green or the error in red below. The avatar colors are eight dark tones that keep white initials readable, plus "Automatic". The danger card has a red border and title; deleting asks for the email typed in the confirm dialog. On narrow pages (by the page width) the links become a strip of chips and controls take the full width.
- Space home: the title block, then tabs on a hairline (Overview, Ideas, Discussions, Documents, Decisions, Tasks; the active one underlined in the accent, counts as small pills; the strip scrolls sideways on phones). Overview opens with Open questions: one warm row each with a "?" mark, the question, who decides (avatar and name), and "Decide by Oct 2", turning red with "Was due" when late. Below, a grid of Open tasks (the task on its own line, then its source, assignee, and date), Recent discussions, Latest decisions, and Documents, then the activity timeline.
- Discussions: a quiet list on hairlines (title, a question badge with owner and date or Answered or Closed, the last message, the people, the count and time), an Open / All switch, and "New discussion" as the one filled button. The new-discussion form is a card: a large title field, the first message, and a "This needs a decision" switch that reveals Who decides and Decide by. A thread shows the title, a question card (warm while waiting, red when late, green when answered, with the answer in a white box), the messages with avatars on hairlines, "Make a task" under a message on hover (always visible on touch screens), the task as a tinted chip with its tick, assignee, and date, and a composer pinned to the bottom with Close or Reopen.
- Ideas: the space's own board, the same columns and cards as a board document (New, Exploring, Picked). The card menu turns an idea into a discussion or, for editors, a document; the card then carries a "Discussion" or "Document" link, and the thread says "From an idea".
- Sign-off: while a document is in review, the Outline tool reads "Sign-off 3/5" (red when a concern is open) and opens the outline as "Outline and sign-off". A line on top counts signed sections and concerns. Under each heading: the people who agree as avatars with a green ring, concerns as red pills with the note, a sign-off on text that changed since faded. Reviewers get Agree (pressed, green, once given) and Concern…, which opens a small note field. A signed section gets a green check at the end of its heading. Reviewers who come from the review queue land with the panel open.
- Decision record: a single reading column. Crumbs (space / Decisions / D-9), an eyebrow "Decision D-9 · space", the title, a status pill and "Decided Sep 23 by Bea". What was decided sits in a green-tinted box with a small green label. Then the chain: "Replaces D-4: …" and "Replaced by D-12" as accent links, and for editors a dashed row "This replaces [select] Save". Then "Why": a line naming the discussion (linked) and who decided by when, and the messages as a transcript on a hairline rail; or, for a document decision, a link to the document. A replaced decision gets an amber banner at the top and its title struck through. The space's Decisions tab is the log: In force / All, each row the number and title as links, the outcome under it, the source (a speech bubble or a page icon with the document name), replaced rows struck and muted with "replaced by D-12". An answered question in a thread links "Recorded as D-9 →"; in the list its badge reads "Decided · D-9". The decision block's "Decision D-9" label links to its record.
- Account menu: the name and email, Settings, and Sign out. The theme moved to Settings; the sidebar has a Settings link at the bottom.
- New menu: Write, Brainstorm, Plan, and Review, each with a one-line description.
- Bell: a popover with a header ("Notifications", "3 new"), then "New" and "Earlier" groups. Each row is a link to the document: the actor's avatar with a small badge for the kind of event, the sentence, and the document name with the time. New rows sit on a light accent wash with a dot. Empty: a bell in an accent circle and "You are all caught up". On phones it is pinned under the top bar at full width.
- Activity: a timeline. "Activity" with the count of updates, day labels on a hairline (Today, Yesterday, then dates), one line running through the avatars, each avatar with the same event badge as the bell (comments in the accent, created and approved in green, sharing in violet, restores in amber, deletes in red), the sentence with the actor in bold, the document as a small outlined chip, and the time on the right. Shown on a space page and under the versions on a history page.

## Accessibility

Every control has a visible focus ring. Icons are decorative; the label next to them carries the meaning, also in the collapsed rail (`aria-label`). Names on cursors are text. The status pill has `role="status"`. The Tab key moves focus and never gets trapped in the editor.
